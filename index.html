Use cases & FAQ

Our Honeywell XNX H2S cartridge is at end of life. Can you supply the exact replacement?

Yes. Send the cartridge part number from the label (for example XNXXSH2SS, the H2S cartridge for XNX) or the transmitter model, gas and range. We quote the exact Honeywell cartridge and confirm availability and lead time with the supplier first. The same applies to MSA Ultima X / X5000 sensor modules and Dräger Polytron sensors.

How often do H2S and CO sensor cartridges need replacing?

Electrochemical H2S and CO sensors typically last about 2–3 years. Actual life depends on the gas, temperature, humidity and exposure, and on the manufacturer's stated life. A sensor that fails calibration or shows an end-of-life fault should be replaced with the part specified by the transmitter manufacturer.

Failed Wilcoxon 786A accelerometer or Bently Nevada 3300 XL probe. Can you match it exactly?

Send the part number from the sensor or cable. Accelerometers are matched on sensitivity (e.g. 100 mV/g), frequency response, connector and mounting thread. Bently Nevada 3300 XL proximity systems are matched on probe, extension cable and Proximitor sensor so that total system length and calibration stay correct. Spec-equivalents (e.g. CTC AC102 for a general-purpose 100 mV/g accelerometer) are only offered when you ask, and they are labelled as equivalents.

The sensor or transmitter has been discontinued. What now?

Discontinued part? Send us the number and we'll source a matching or direct replacement. We check for the manufacturer's successor part (for example a newer cartridge or sensor module), remaining stock, or a spec-equivalent, and state clearly which one is quoted.

How fast will I get a quote, and how do I pay?

We typically reply with a quote within a few hours during business hours. For common cartridges and standard models we can often respond faster. The quote arrives by email. No payment is taken on the website: if you accept, you pay via a payment link or invoice we send, then we order from the supplier.

Do you hold stock? Who ships, and what do I get with the quote?

No stock. Each request is sourced from authorized or specialist industrial suppliers. The quote states manufacturer, part number, whether it is OEM or a labeled equivalent, lead time and price. Pricing, availability and technical suitability are confirmed with the supplier before any order. After payment we order from the supplier, and the supplier ships direct to your delivery location.
