Use cases & FAQ

Failed 4-20 mA velocity transmitter on a pump – can you supply the exact OEM part?

Send the part number from the nameplate. We quote the exact OEM unit where available. If it is obsolete, we propose a spec-equivalent matched on full-scale range (in/s or mm/s, peak or RMS), sensitivity, frequency response, connector, mounting thread and hazardous-area rating, and identify it as an equivalent on the quote.

H2S transmitter in a Class I Div 1 area needs a like-for-like replacement.

Enter gas, range, output (4-20 mA, HART, Modbus) and the required certification. Units are quoted only after the supplier confirms the rating (e.g. UL/CSA Class I Div 1 Groups B-D, ATEX/IECEx zone and gas group, SIL) matches your requirement.

Refrigerant leak-detection module in a machinery room or A2L system must be replaced.

Give refrigerant (e.g. R-744, R-717, R-410A, R-32, R-454B), ppm range, alarm set-points, output and the existing model or controller. We source the matching module or the compatible plug-in sensor head so the existing BMS/controller wiring stays unchanged.

Do you hold stock? Who ships, and what do I get with the quote?

No stock. Each request is sourced from authorized or specialist industrial suppliers and shipped direct from the supplier to your delivery location. The quote states manufacturer, part number, whether it is OEM or equivalent, lead time and price. Pricing, availability and technical suitability are confirmed with the supplier before any order.
